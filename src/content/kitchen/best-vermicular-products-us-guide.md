## What Is Available in the US vs. Japan

Vermicular sells through official channels in the US: the brand's own website (vermicular.us),
authorized Amazon.com listings under the Vermicular USA seller name, and Williams-Sonoma in
select stores. The US lineup is narrower than the Japan catalog.

Products confirmed available in the US as of August 2026:
- Oven Pot (original) starting at $120
- Oven Pot 2.0 in multiple sizes starting at $160
- Frying Pan starting at $150
- Musui-Kamado at $670

Products in the Japan catalog not found in US listings as of August 2026:
- Santoku kitchen knife (launched Japan-only, June 2026)
- Yukihira pot (traditional Japanese saucepan style)
- Deep bowl tableware line

## The Uncoated Rim: What to Know Before You Buy

Every Oven Pot model has an uncoated iron rim -- the contact surface between the lid and body
is left without enamel coating so the precision machining tolerances can function as intended.
This is not a defect; it is the design that enables the 0.01mm lid-to-body fit that Vermicular
cites as the basis for waterless cooking.

The practical implication for care: the rim needs to be dried completely after washing and
periodically treated with a thin layer of cooking oil to prevent rust. Reviewers describe this
as a routine maintenance step, not a major inconvenience, but it is a real difference from a
fully enameled Le Creuset or Staub where no part of the iron is exposed.

## How to Identify an Official vs. Gray Market Listing

On Amazon, check the seller name on the product detail page before purchasing. Official
Vermicular listings are sold by "Vermicular USA." Third-party sellers sometimes list older
Japan-market Vermicular models, identifiable by their model number format (PNK26R, BRN26R,
NBG22R) and product titles that include the phrase "Anhydrous Cooking" -- a literal translation
of Musui that Vermicular USA does not use in its official US product naming.

Purchases from third-party sellers of Japan-market models fall outside Vermicular's 3-year
warranty, which covers only products purchased from authorized US and Canadian retailers.
A dedicated gray market listing guide on this site covers this in more detail.

## Who Should Buy the Musui-Kamado

The Musui-Kamado at $670 is in a different category from the Oven Pot. It is an electric
precision cooker that automates heat control for waterless cooking and rice functions. The
Japan-market equivalent (Rice Pot) is priced near JPY 95,370 -- approximately comparable
at August 2026 exchange rates, which means the US price is not a significant markup over
the domestic Japanese market price.

The Musui-Kamado makes sense for buyers who cook in this specific style daily and want
the automation rather than manual stovetop control. For buyers who occasionally braise or
want a Dutch oven for general use, the Oven Pot at a fraction of the price is the
appropriate product. See the separate Musui-Kamado guide on this site for a full breakdown.
