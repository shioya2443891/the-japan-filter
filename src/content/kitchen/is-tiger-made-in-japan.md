## A Japanese Company Isn't the Same as Japan-Made Products

"Is Tiger a Japanese brand?" and "Is this specific Tiger product made in Japan?" get treated as the same question. They're not. Tiger Corporation is unambiguously headquartered in Osaka -- that's settled. Where any individual product in its catalog is manufactured is a separate, model-specific fact that has to be checked on its own, especially given the company's multi-country manufacturing footprint.

## What's Actually Confirmed

Based on the official Amazon listings reviewed for this guide, Tiger's rice cooker lines -- JBV-S Micom, JKT-D and JPW-H IH -- state Made in Japan explicitly in the product title. That's a direct, title-level confirmation, not something inferred from brand marketing.

## What's Not Confirmed -- and Why That's Not "No"

The stainless bottle lines reviewed (MCZ, MTA-T) did not have a Made in Japan claim in the official pages checked. That's a gap in what this research found, not confirmation those products are manufactured elsewhere. Given Tiger's manufacturing operations across Taiwan, China, Vietnam, and India, the honest answer is that country of manufacture varies by product line, and an unconfirmed listing should be checked directly rather than assumed either way.

## The One Model Confirmed Down to Its Artisan Source

The clearest Made in Japan case in Tiger's entire catalog wasn't found in the US listings reviewed: the JRT-A100 donabe-style flagship, whose inner pot is produced specifically by Banko-ware artisans in Yokkaichi, Mie Prefecture. It's a useful reference point for how specific Tiger's Japan-manufacturing claims can get -- just not one that appeared in Tiger's official US Amazon listings at the time of our research (August 2026).
