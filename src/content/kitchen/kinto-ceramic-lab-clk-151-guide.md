## What CERAMIC LAB Actually Is

CERAMIC LAB, identified by the CLK-151 model code, is KINTO's ceramic tableware line, distinct from the brand's better-known glass and stainless steel drinkware. It is manufactured in Hasami, a town in Nagasaki Prefecture with a porcelain-production history documented at more than 400 years. KINTO has operated a dedicated product center in Hasami since 1999 to manage quality control for this specific line -- a level of operational commitment to a single production region that the rest of KINTO's catalog, sourced from China for glass and steel items, does not carry.

## The Price Gap Is Real, But Different From the Tumbler Story

Like the Travel Tumbler, CLK-151 plates cost meaningfully more in the US than in Japan -- roughly 66% more for the 160mm plate and roughly 73% more for the 250mm plate, based on the domestic and US listings reviewed for this guide. The difference is what that premium is attached to: a Japan-manufactured product with a documented production region, not a China-made item being resold at an import markup. Whether that distinction changes the purchase decision depends on how much a buyer specifically values Japan-made ceramics versus KINTO's design regardless of manufacturing origin.

## Hasami vs. More Familiar Ceramics Regions

Buyers more familiar with Arita ware, Mino ware, or other widely marketed Japanese ceramics regions may not have an existing reference point for Hasami. That's a gap in general US market awareness, not a gap in Hasami's production history -- the region has centuries of documented ceramics production, but it has not been marketed internationally to the same degree as some other regions. Buyers specifically drawn to the CERAMIC LAB line for its Hasami connection may want to research the region independently rather than relying solely on KINTO's own framing.

## Who Should Buy Into This Line

CERAMIC LAB works for buyers who want a specific, verifiable Japan-made component in their kitchen -- daily-use tableware with a documented production region -- rather than buyers who are drawn to KINTO primarily for its drinkware or its association with US specialty coffee culture, where the Travel Tumbler and CAST lines (both China-made) are the more relevant products.
