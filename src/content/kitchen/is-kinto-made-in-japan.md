## Two Different Claims, Often Treated as One

"KINTO is a Japanese brand" and "this specific KINTO product is manufactured in Japan" are related but separate facts. The first is well documented: KINTO was founded in Hikone, Shiga Prefecture in 1972 and remains headquartered in Japan. The second depends entirely on which product line you're looking at.

## What's Specifically Confirmed as Japan-Made

Based on kinto-usa.com's own product specifications, the CERAMIC LAB line -- plates, bowls, and other tableware under the CLK-151 designation -- is manufactured in Hasami, Nagasaki Prefecture. KINTO has operated a dedicated product center in Hasami since 1999 specifically to manage quality control for this line. Hasami is a porcelain-producing region with a documented history of more than 400 years. This is the most specific, regionally confirmed part of KINTO's catalog.

## What's Specifically Confirmed as China-Made

The Travel Tumbler and CAST double-wall glass lines are manufactured in China, according to the same official product specifications. KINTO discloses this directly on its own product pages -- the China-made status is a confirmed specification for both lines, not an unstated or inferred detail. The Travel Tumbler's US visibility has a well-documented connection to Blue Bottle Coffee's publicly noted use of KINTO carafes in its US cafes.

## Why the Split Exists

KINTO's own company history explains part of this: the company began in 1972 as a tableware wholesale business, not a manufacturer, and only started developing original products in 1982. That wholesale-and-design origin is different from a company like Hario, which began as a glassware manufacturer and later expanded into coffee equipment using its own factory. KINTO's model -- design in-house, manufacture through a mix of Japanese regional partners (for ceramics) and overseas facilities (for glass and steel) -- is a structural difference in how the two companies operate, not a quality signal in either direction.

## A Practical Rule for Checking Any KINTO Product

1. Check the specific product line, not just the KINTO brand name -- CERAMIC LAB and drinkware (Travel Tumbler, CAST) have different confirmed origins.
2. Look for a named production region (Hasami, Nagasaki Prefecture) rather than a generic "Japan" or "imported" label -- KINTO's own pages provide this level of detail for CERAMIC LAB.
3. A China-made KINTO product is a disclosed specification, not a discovered discrepancy -- it does not indicate an authenticity problem.
4. If Made in Japan status is a deciding factor for your purchase, CERAMIC LAB is the line to choose; other KINTO lines outside CERAMIC LAB, Travel Tumbler, and CAST were not checked for this guide and should be verified individually.
