## The Price Comparison

Japan price: JPY 33,660 for the Oven Pot 2.0 22cm, after the August 2025 revision.

At 153 JPY per USD (approximate August 2026 rate), this converts to approximately $220.

US price range: around $160-220 through official US channels (Vermicular USA on Amazon,
vermicular.us, Williams-Sonoma).

The overlap is significant. Japan is not meaningfully cheaper.

Before August 2025, the Japan price at JPY 29,700 converted to approximately $194 at the
same rate -- a more meaningful difference. The August 2025 revision brought the Japan price
up, narrowing the gap to where US retail pricing is essentially equivalent.

## The Tourist Tax Refund Scenario

Japan offers a consumption tax refund (currently 10% of the purchase price) to foreign
tourists who purchase eligible products. For an Oven Pot 2.0 22cm at JPY 33,660, the tax
component is approximately JPY 3,060 (around $20). Claiming the refund requires purchasing
at a participating retailer, completing the refund documentation at customs when leaving
Japan, and in some cases following up after return.

Including the refund, the effective Japan price drops to approximately JPY 30,600, or around
$200. This is at the midpoint of the US retail range and below the higher end of US pricing.
The effective savings for a buyer who successfully claims the refund: somewhere between zero
and $20, depending on where in the US price range they would otherwise have purchased.

That savings does not include:

- Any international shipping cost if ordering for delivery (not a Japan trip purchase)
- Weight consideration for checked luggage -- an enameled cast iron Dutch oven weighs
  approximately 2.7kg and must be carefully packed to avoid breakage
- The 3-year US warranty, which would be forfeited on a Japan purchase

## What to Buy Instead

Buy through official US channels. The same Vermicular product is available with full
3-year warranty coverage through Amazon (Vermicular USA), vermicular.us, or Williams-Sonoma.
The Oven Pot 2.0 22cm is the recommended starting point for most households. See the full
product guide on this site for details on which size and model suits your needs.
