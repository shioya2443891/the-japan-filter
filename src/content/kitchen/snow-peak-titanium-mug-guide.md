## The Two Variables That Actually Matter

Size (300ml vs. 450ml) determines how much the mug holds -- 300ml suits a quick espresso-style drink or minimal pack weight, while 450ml covers a full mug of coffee or tea. Wall construction (single vs. double) determines insulation -- double-wall keeps drinks hot or cold longer but adds roughly $15-20 to the price and a bit more weight and bulk.

## Matching the Mug to the Use Case

For ultralight backpacking where every gram counts, the Ti-Single 300 is the lightest, cheapest option. For a general camping mug that covers a full drink without insulation, the Ti-Single 450 is the best value. For car camping or daily desk/home use where insulation matters more than pack weight, the Ti-Double 450 is the pick most reviewers describe as getting the most actual use. The Ti-Double 300 sits in between -- insulated, but more compact than the 450 size.

## One Pricing Detail Worth Knowing

It would be reasonable to assume that, being a Japan-made product, these mugs are cheaper to buy while traveling in Japan. For the Ti-Single 450 specifically, that's not the case -- Japan's own official store price for that model converts to more than the US retail price. There's no blanket savings case for buying the standard mug lineup in Japan; the items actually worth seeking out during Japan travel are the flagship-store-exclusive colorways covered separately on this site, not the standard catalog covered in this guide.
