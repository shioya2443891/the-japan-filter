## Why IH Is the Real Top Tier for US Buyers

Tiger's Japan domestic lineup places IH in the middle of a four-tier structure, below Pressure IH and the donabe-style flagship. Neither of those upper tiers is sold through official US channels based on this research, which makes IH the genuine ceiling for US buyers rather than a mid-range compromise.

## The Inner Pot Difference, Specifically

The JKT-D series uses a multi-layer ceramic coating on its inner pot; the newer JPW-H series, launched in 2024, uses a thicker far-infrared ceramic coating, per the manufacturer specifications reviewed. That's a genuine material difference between the two series, not just a new model number on the same hardware.

## Pricing: What's Confirmed and What Isn't

Third-party retail pricing for the JKT-D series was confirmed during this research; the JPW-H series' pricing was not stated on the official product page reviewed. Confirm current pricing directly on the Amazon listing for either series before deciding based on price.

## Which Series to Choose

If confirmed pricing and an established track record matter more, the JKT-D series is the safer starting point. If the newer inner pot coating is the deciding specification for you, the JPW-H series is the more recent design -- just verify current pricing directly, since it wasn't stated on the official page reviewed for this guide.
