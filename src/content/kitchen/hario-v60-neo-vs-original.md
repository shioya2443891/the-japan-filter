## Twenty Years, One Design Change

The Hario V60 has used the same spiral rib pattern inside its cone since it launched in 2005. That's unusually long for a product this central to a company's identity -- most competing drippers have gone through multiple design iterations in the same period. In 2025, Hario released the V60 NEO with a different interior structure: ultra-fine vertical ribbing instead of the original spiral pattern. In June 2026, the company added two new colorways to the NEO line in a global, simultaneous launch.

## What Actually Changed

The rib pattern is the core difference. The original V60's spiral ribbing creates a specific pattern of contact and airflow between the filter and the dripper wall, and two decades of brewing recipes and ratios in the specialty coffee community are calibrated to that exact geometry. The NEO's ultra-fine vertical ribs are described in manufacturer and product coverage as changing that contact pattern, which affects flow rate during a pour.

The material changed too. The NEO's plastic version uses Tritan resin, a BPA-free material, rather than the original's heat-resistant plastic. That's a genuine material difference, not just a marketing label -- Tritan is generally more transparent and more heat-tolerant than standard heat-resistant plastics used in kitchenware.

## What Didn't Change

The overall cone shape and the single large hole at the base are consistent between the original and the NEO. Both use the same fundamental pour-over method. And critically, Hario has not discontinued the original V60 in favor of the NEO -- both are current products, sold side by side.

## Who Should Actually Switch

If you already have a V60 and a brewing routine you're happy with, there's no discontinuation pressure forcing an upgrade -- the original design remains fully available and supported. If you're buying your first V60, or you're the kind of buyer who wants the current design Hario is actively developing, the NEO is a reasonable starting point, especially for buyers who value the Tritan resin's clarity and heat tolerance over the original plastic. Buyers deeply invested in existing spiral-rib brewing recipes should be aware that switching to the NEO's vertical ribbing may require re-calibrating pour speed and ratio, since community guides written for the original design won't map exactly onto the new geometry.
