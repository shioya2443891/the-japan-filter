## Two Companies, One Competitive Market

Tiger and Zojirushi's rivalry isn't limited to rice cookers -- both brands sell stainless bottles and mugs through official US Amazon storefronts, and both trace back to the same competitive Japan domestic market that has pushed insulated drinkware toward slimmer builds and more precise lids over decades.

## Capacity and Lid Mechanism: The Real Comparison Points

The two models here aren't a direct size match -- the Tiger MCZ-S060 holds 20oz with a screw cap, while the Zojirushi SM-KHE36 holds 12oz with a flip-open lid. That's a genuine functional difference, not just a spec-sheet number: a flip-open lid is faster for one-handed office use, while a larger screw-cap bottle holds more for a longer day away from a refill source.

## What the Japan-US Price Gap Actually Means

The roughly 52% gap between the Tiger MCZ-S060's Japan domestic price and its official US price is consistent with import and distribution markup, based on the pricing reviewed. It isn't evidence of a different or upgraded product sold specifically to the US market.

## Picking Between Them

Choose based on capacity and lid mechanism first, brand second. If you want a larger everyday bottle with a simple screw cap, the Tiger MCZ-S060 fits. If a smaller, flip-open desk mug matters more, the Zojirushi SM-KHE36 is the better functional match -- and it's worth checking Zojirushi's broader bottle and mug catalog, since the SM line is only one of several structurally different lines the brand sells.
