## What the 0.01mm Tolerance Actually Means

Vermicular's central product claim is that the lid and body of the Oven Pot are machined to
a contact gap of under 0.01mm. The company states this precision is what makes waterless
cooking possible -- it traps steam inside the pot instead of allowing it to escape from the
gap between lid and rim.

For context, a standard sheet of printer paper is approximately 0.1mm thick. The claimed
Vermicular tolerance is tighter than a tenth of that gap. Aichi Dobby, the manufacturer,
developed this precision machining in the context of industrial hydraulic components for
marine and construction equipment before applying the same approach to cookware. The
claim is traceable to a specific manufacturing origin rather than being a general brand
assertion.

The practical result, as reviewers commonly describe it: moisture that would normally
escape as steam is retained inside the pot, concentrating flavors and allowing cooking
with little or no added liquid. This is the technique called Musui -- waterless cooking --
and it is what distinguishes the Oven Pot from a standard Dutch oven where the lid fit
is less precise.

## The Uncoated Rim: What It Is and Why It Exists

The lid and body of the Oven Pot meet at a rim that is not enameled. This is the most
commonly noted difference between Vermicular and competitors like Le Creuset and Staub,
which enamel the entire surface including the rim.

The absence of enamel at the rim is intentional: the enamel layer would vary in thickness
across the contact surface and break the 0.01mm tolerance. Leaving the rim unenameled is
what makes the precision fit possible. The tradeoff is that bare iron at the rim will rust
if left wet.

Care requirement: after washing, dry the pot completely including the rim, then apply a
thin layer of cooking oil to any exposed iron. Reviewers consistently describe this as a
quick step that takes under a minute and becomes automatic after a few cycles of use.
Buyers who prefer to air-dry cookware without additional steps, or who find this type of
maintenance inconvenient, should consider Le Creuset or Staub instead.

## Size Guide

Vermicular sells the Oven Pot 2.0 in multiple sizes. The 22cm (2.4qt) version is the
most commonly discussed in US reviews and suits households of 2-4 people for soups,
braises, and rice-based dishes based on reviewer descriptions of typical use.

The 26cm version provides more capacity for larger households or for batch cooking.
An 18cm version covers single-person or side-dish applications.

## How to Buy

Official US sales channels for the Oven Pot 2.0 are vermicular.us, Amazon.com (seller:
Vermicular USA), and Williams-Sonoma in select US locations. Purchases through these
channels carry Vermicular's 3-year warranty for US buyers.

Third-party Amazon listings for legacy Japan-market Vermicular models also exist. These
are identifiable by model numbers in the format PNK26R or BRN26R and product titles using
the phrase "Anhydrous Cooking." These are not official US products and fall outside
Vermicular's warranty scope. See the separate gray market guide on this site for how to
