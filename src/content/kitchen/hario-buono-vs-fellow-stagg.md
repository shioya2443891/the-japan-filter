## Two Different Design Philosophies

Fellow's Stagg line is built around precision -- digital temperature settings, timers, and a design language aimed at buyers who want the kettle itself to manage brewing variables. Hario's Buono line, across both its stovetop and electric versions, is a simpler tool: a controlled pour spout without the same built-in digital feature set, at a correspondingly lower price.

## Stovetop: Buono's Clear Advantage

At the stovetop tier, the comparison isn't close on price. The Buono stovetop kettle costs roughly a third of what Fellow's comparable stovetop offering runs, based on the US pricing reviewed for this guide, and it does the one thing a gooseneck kettle needs to do: control pour rate and direction. Neither stovetop kettle in this category typically includes built-in temperature readout, so buyers who care about precise water temperature need a separate thermometer regardless of which stovetop kettle they choose.

## Electric: Where the Price Gap Gets Real

The gap widens significantly once you move to electric kettles. The Buono Electric runs around $95, while the Fellow Stagg EKG runs $149 to $200 depending on the listing. That difference is largely explained by the EKG's built-in precision digital temperature control and timer -- features that, based on the sources reviewed, the Buono Electric does not match. If those specific features matter to your brewing routine, the price gap reflects a real functional difference, not just a brand premium.

## The Japan Price Gap Worth Knowing About

Hario's Buono stovetop kettle sells for roughly 4,180 to 4,730 yen at the company's official Japan online shop -- a price that, even accounting for unconfirmed exchange-rate conversion, appears well below the US list price of $60-65. This isn't a loophole US buyers can access; based on available information, this appears to reflect standard import and market pricing differences between the two retail channels. It's useful context for understanding why the US price is what it is, not a reason to expect a discount.

## Which One to Buy

If you don't need built-in temperature control, the Buono stovetop kettle is the lower-risk, lower-cost choice, and it will do the core job of a gooseneck kettle well. If you want the convenience of an electric, cordless kettle without paying for precision temperature control you may not use, the Buono Electric is the better value. If precise temperature settings and a built-in timer are non-negotiable for your brewing routine, the Fellow Stagg EKG's premium reflects a real feature gap, not just a US design brand markup.
