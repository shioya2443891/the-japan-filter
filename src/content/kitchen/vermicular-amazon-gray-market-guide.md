## The Gray Market Is Not a Counterfeit Market

Vermicular products on Amazon from third-party sellers are, as far as the research reviewed
for this guide indicates, genuine Vermicular products made by Aichi Dobby Corporation in
Nagoya. They are not fakes. The gray market problem is different: these are Japan-market
models sold in the US outside Vermicular's authorized distribution channel.

This distinction matters for how you evaluate the purchase. The product itself is real.
The issue is what you lose by buying it: warranty coverage.

## How to Read an Amazon Listing

When you search "Vermicular" on Amazon, results may include both official Vermicular USA
listings and third-party listings for Japan-market models. They may appear on the same
search results page. Here is how to tell them apart:

**On the product detail page, look for the seller information.** The section that shows
"Sold by" and "Ships from" is what you need. Official Vermicular listings on Amazon are
sold by "Vermicular USA." If you see any other seller name, it is a third-party listing.

**Check the model number.** Japan-market Vermicular models use a format of color code +
size number + shape letter: PNK26R (Pearl Pink, 26cm, Round), BRN26R (Pearl Brown, 26cm,
Round), NBG22R (Natural Beige, 22cm, Round). If the model number in the listing follows
this format, it is a Japan-market model. Vermicular USA's official US listings use different
naming conventions.

**Check the product title.** Japan-market listings commonly use the phrase "Anhydrous
Cooking" in the product title. This is a direct translation of Musui (waterless cooking)
that Vermicular USA does not use in its official US product naming. If the title says
"Anhydrous Cooking," this is a third-party Japan-market listing.

## What to Buy Instead

Official Vermicular products with US warranty coverage are available through:

- Amazon.com (seller: Vermicular USA)
- vermicular.us (official US direct store)
- Williams-Sonoma (select US locations, per Vermicular's official help center)
