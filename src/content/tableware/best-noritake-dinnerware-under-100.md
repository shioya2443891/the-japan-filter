## What You're Actually Getting Under $100

The $50窶・00 range for Noritake Colorwave covers the casual stoneware line 窶・not the formal bone china that Noritake built its name on in Japan. That distinction matters for setting expectations: Colorwave is everyday dinnerware with a durable matte glaze and a wide color range, not a formal set for entertaining.

What you get at this price point that you don't get with generic alternatives: an official US storefront (Noritake U.S.A., Inc.) that removes the third-party reseller uncertainty common in unfamiliar import brands, and a review record that covers 12+ months of regular dishwasher use across a large verified purchase base.

## The 16-Piece vs. 20-Piece Question
