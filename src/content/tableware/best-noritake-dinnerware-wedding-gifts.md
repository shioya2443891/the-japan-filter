Noritake has been making tableware in Nagoya since 1904, and it remains one of the
few Japanese dinnerware brands with a fully official presence on Amazon.com —
sold directly by Noritake U.S.A., Inc. rather than third-party resellers. That
matters for a gift purchase: you're not gambling on an unfamiliar import.

This guide focuses on sets in the $50–150 range, since that's the band most
wedding and housewarming gift-givers are actually searching in — not the
