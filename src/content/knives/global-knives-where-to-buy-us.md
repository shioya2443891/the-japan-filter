## Why the Seller Name Matters

Most Amazon product pages for popular items show a default seller with the option to switch to other sellers at the same or lower prices. For Global Knives, that default seller matters more than usual.

Global Cutlery USA's authorized retailer policy, which we reviewed directly, prohibits third-party sellers from listing Global knives on Amazon.com or Amazon.ca without prior written consent from SCANPAN USA Inc. That policy means a lower-priced listing from a third-party seller may not be an authorized sale -- and a knife purchased from an unauthorized seller is not covered under the US warranty terms we reviewed.

## The Authorized Channel Is Clear

The 'Global Cutlery' brand store on Amazon.com is operated by SCANPAN USA Inc., which holds the exclusive US sales rights for Global Knives as Global Cutlery USA. Purchasing from this store is the most straightforward authorized US route. The globalcutleryusa.com direct site is another authorized option, operated by the same distributor.

## The Distributor Structure Is Unusual

The distributor situation is worth understanding: Yoshikin (the Japanese manufacturer) has its global distribution rights held by Master Cutlery, and SCANPAN USA -- a US subsidiary of Danish cookware company SCANPAN A/S -- holds the exclusive US sales rights. A US buyer purchasing Global Knives is transacting with a Danish company's US subsidiary, not with the Japanese manufacturer.

This is not a problem in itself -- the product is still made by Yoshikin in Tsubame City -- but it explains why warranty service goes through SCANPAN USA (scanpanwarranty@scanpan.com, 1-877-772-5467) rather than through Yoshikin.

## Counterfeit Risk on Other Platforms

Yoshikin has issued an official warning about counterfeit Global knives on eBay, describing them as arriving in branded knife rolls and sold under names like "GLOBAL Chef" or "GLOBAL Professional." These names do not correspond to any actual Global product line. When purchasing outside Amazon or the direct Global Cutlery USA site, the counterfeit risk increases.

For a US buyer who wants a straightforward purchase with warranty coverage, the Global Cutlery brand store on Amazon.com is the cleaner option. See our [Global Knives Japan vs. US price guide](/knives/global-knives-japan-vs-us-price/) for how the cost compares to buying in Japan.
