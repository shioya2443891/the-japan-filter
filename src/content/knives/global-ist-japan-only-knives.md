## Two Lines from One Manufacturer

Yoshikin makes two separate knife systems under the Global brand name. The G-Series -- launched in 1985 and designed from the start for Western export markets -- is the line sold through Global Cutlery USA and the Global Cutlery brand store on Amazon.com. GLOBAL-IST is a parallel line developed for Japanese home cooks, available through Yoshikin's Japan retail channels, and carrying the tagline "Made in Japan, Made for Japan."

The two lines share the same manufacturer, the same Tsubame City production base, and the same Cromova 18 stainless steel. What differs is the purpose the design serves.

## What GLOBAL-IST Is

GLOBAL-IST covers shapes that reflect Japanese home kitchen practice: santoku in two compact sizes (14cm and 16.5cm), gyuto (18cm and 20cm), nakiri, a deba, a yanagiba, and a bread knife. The pricing in Japan is lower than the G-Series -- approximately 5,940 yen (around $40) for the IST santoku versus 12,100 yen ($82) for the G-2 -- which reflects the mass-home-market positioning rather than the design-forward export premium.

The IST is also designed for Japan's smaller home kitchen context: compact blade lengths, a lighter construction, and shapes that match the types of cooking Japanese home cooks do most frequently (fish preparation, vegetable work, daily meal prep).

## The Double-Bevel Deba and Yanagiba

The most technically interesting detail in the GLOBAL-IST line is that its deba and yanagiba knives are double-bevel. This is not the professional standard.

In a Japanese professional kitchen -- a sushi restaurant, a Japanese-style butcher, a traditional kaiseki kitchen -- a deba (for cutting fish through bone) and a yanagiba (for sashimi slicing) are typically single-bevel: ground flat on one face, with a concave back, optimized for one-handed technique and right-handed use. Single-bevel tools require a specific sharpening approach (flat-face maintenance plus bevel sharpening) and produce a cutting edge geometry that differs from a double-bevel knife.

The GLOBAL-IST deba and yanagiba use double-bevel construction throughout the line, per the manufacturer specifications reviewed. This is a deliberate choice for Japanese home cooks who want the shape and basic function of these traditional forms without the single-bevel maintenance requirements and right-handed limitation. A home cook making occasional fish preparations does not need the precision edge geometry a professional sashimi chef requires; the IST double-bevel versions serve the same general purpose with a simpler sharpening process.

## US Availability

During research for this article (August 2026), GLOBAL-IST was not found in the Global Cutlery brand store on Amazon.com, and was not identified in the globalcutleryusa.com product catalog. US authorized availability through SCANPAN USA's distribution channels could not be confirmed.

For US buyers who encounter GLOBAL-IST through third-party Amazon sellers or import channels, the standard authorized-seller evaluation applies: Global Cutlery USA's warranty does not cover knives purchased from unauthorized channels. For IST specifically, even the brand store does not appear to stock the line, making the warranty question distinct from the G-Series case. The clearest purchase route for IST is directly in Japan through Yoshikin's authorized retail channels (Roppongi flagship, department stores, Tokyu Hands, Loft, or 212 Kitchen Store).
