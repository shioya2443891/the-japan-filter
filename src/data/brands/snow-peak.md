## LEVEL 1 -- Confirmed from primary sources (2026-08-17)

- Founded 1958 in Sanjo City, Niigata Prefecture, by Yukio Yamai as Yamai Shoten.
  Source: Wikipedia "Snow Peak (company)" (2026-08-17)
- Brand name "Snow Peak" adopted in 1963, named after Mt. Tanigawa (Gunma Prefecture).
  Source: Wikipedia (2026-08-17)
- First products were crampons and pitons in stainless steel and titanium.
  Source: Wikipedia (2026-08-17)
- Headquartered in Sanjo City, part of the Tsubame-Sanjo metalworking industrial region.
  Source: Wikipedia + multiple web sources (2026-08-17)
- Amazon.com official brand store confirmed across multiple category pages (Cookware, Outdoor Living, Sports & Outdoors).
  Source: Amazon.com store pages (2026-08-17)
- Official sites: snowpeak.com (US), ec.snowpeak.co.jp (Japan)
- US flagship retail stores: Portland (OR), Brooklyn (NY), Seattle (WA).
  Source: Wikipedia (2026-08-17)
- Lifetime Product Guarantee applies to all hardware products.
  Source: snowpeak.com/pages/faq-2 (2026-08-17)
- Japan domestic price revision (effective 2025-12-13):
  - Entry IGT: JPY 23,650 -> JPY 18,480 (-22%)
  - Low Chair 30: JPY 22,000 -> JPY 17,600 (-20%)
  - Bonfire Stand M Starter Set: JPY 27,390 -> JPY 21,780 (-20%)
  - Bonfire Stand L Starter Set: JPY 33,990 -> JPY 27,280 (-20%)
  - One-Action Low Table Bamboo: JPY 33,000 -> JPY 27,500 (-17%)
  Source: campreview.jp (2026-08-17)
- Location-limited products exist: anodized titanium colorways sold only at flagship stores/campgrounds, Local Wear apparel line, a Kyoto-limited Kaikado collaboration coffee canister, region-specific tenugui towels.
  Source: korogi.co (2026-08-17)
- Japan's official EC store offers a name-engraving service on titanium products.
  Source: giftmall.co.jp search results (2026-08-17)

## Tsubame-Sanjo manufacturing connection

**LEVEL 1**
- Sanjo City is part of the Tsubame-Sanjo metalworking industrial region of Niigata Prefecture.
- Global Knives (Yoshida Metal Industry) is based in neighboring Tsubame City, same broader metalworking region.
- Snow Peak (Sanjo, tool/blacksmith lineage) and Global (Tsubame, Western tableware lineage) come from historically distinct sub-industries within the same district.

**LEVEL 2**
- Multiple industry articles credit the Tsubame-Sanjo metalworking base as the technical foundation for Snow Peak's titanium precision manufacturing.

## Warranty terms (verified 2026-08-17, primary source)

- Snow Peak's Lifetime Product Guarantee: "repair it or replace it free of charge" if a product
  fails to meet the company's standards (snowpeak.com/pages/warranty-repair)
- Excludes: everyday wear and tear (scuffs/scratches, abrasions, faded colors, minor denting),
  accidental damage from misuse, and certain high-wear components sold separately
- Applies only to products purchased from Snow Peak directly or from authorized Snow Peak dealers
- Proof of purchase required; warranty applies to the original owner only
- Snow Peak USA does not accept returns for items shipped internationally
- International customers are responsible for customs, duties, and VAT on warranty/repair claims
- The warranty page does not state a specific "US/Canada only" geographic restriction the way
  Vermicular's does -- the operative condition is "authorized dealer," not country of purchase.
  This is a meaningfully different (and less US-buyer-adverse) warranty structure than the
  Vermicular precedent on this site, and articles should not assume the same risk framing.
