## Brand Historical Background (Core Japan Context Material)

**LEVEL 1 -- Confirmed from primary sources**

Aichi Dobby was founded in Nagoya in 1936 as a foundry manufacturer of parts for industrial
textile machinery (dobby looms). As Japan's textile industry declined and overseas manufacturers
undercut domestic producers, the company approached near-bankruptcy.

Kunihiro Hijikata (eldest son) left his position as a forex dealer at Toyota Tsusho to join the
family business (2001). Tomoharu Hijikata (younger son) followed from a cost management role at
Toyota (2006). Kunihiro took the foundry craft role; Tomoharu the precision machining role.
Development of consumer cookware began in 2007. After three years of development, the Vermicular
Oven Pot launched in February 2010.

The structure is: a near-bankrupt industrial foundry transferred hydraulic component precision
machining to cookware. This provides a verifiable, specific Japan Context -- not "Japanese quality
in general" but a 0.01mm tolerance figure derived directly from an identifiable manufacturing heritage.
